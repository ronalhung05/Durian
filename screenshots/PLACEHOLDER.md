## Taking Screenshots

### For Android Emulator:
- Click the camera icon in the emulator toolbar, or
- Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + S` (Mac)
- Screenshots are saved to your desktop by default

### For iOS Simulator:
- Press `Cmd + S`
- Screenshots are saved to your desktop by default

### For Physical Devices:
- **Android**: Volume Down + Power button
- **iOS**: Side Button + Volume Up button

After taking screenshots, transfer them to this directory and rename them according to the naming convention in README.md.
